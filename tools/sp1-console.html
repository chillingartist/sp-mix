Faders

Move each of the four faders to the top and to the bottom. The dashed lines mark the lowest and highest raw values seen since connecting. The firmware maps 60–3600 to the full range (measured: 0 at the bottom, about 3700–3820 at the top).

Button test

Hold the button named in the highlighted row for about 1 second, then release it. The reading is recorded on release and the next row is selected. For combinations the order does not matter. Click any row to test it again. "Expected" is the firmware's band for that button; "Decoded as" is what the firmware's thresholds make of the reading.
